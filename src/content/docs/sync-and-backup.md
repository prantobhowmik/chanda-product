Chanda is offline-first. Entries save on the phone first, and the app never waits on the network.

## Automatic sync

Changes sync to the cloud automatically in the background. Internet is needed for sign in, sync and backup only. Recording works offline after sign in.

## New device

Sign in on a new device and all your records are restored.
