A festival is a fund or event with its own balance. Every donation and expense you record belongs to a festival.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows three numbers for each festival:

- **Total collected**: the donations recorded.
- **Total spent**: the expenses recorded.
- **Current balance**: what is left.

## Carry a balance forward

When a festival ends, admins can carry its leftover balance into a new festival, so the money is accounted for from one festival to the next.
