Every team member has one of three roles: Admin, Cashier or Member.

| Role | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | Invites members by email, approves or rejects submissions, approves archive requests from non-admins, restores archived items, carries a festival's balance forward and renames the team. |
| Cashier | One of the three team roles, alongside Admin and Member. |
| Member | Submits donations and expenses for admin approval. Can request an invitation. |

## Invites

Admins invite members by email. Members can request an invitation.
