## The APK will not install

- Allow your browser to install apps from this source when Android asks.
- Android warns about apps from outside the Play Store. That is expected for a direct APK install, because Chanda is not on the Play Store.
- Check that your phone meets the minimum Android version listed on the [Download page](/download).

## Sync is not updating

Sync needs an internet connection. Entries you record offline are saved on the phone and sync in the background once you are online. Records you have already synced are restored when you sign in on a new device.

## I want to change my password

Use Change password in the app.
