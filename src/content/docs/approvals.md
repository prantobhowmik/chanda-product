Members submit donations and expenses. Admins approve or reject them.

## For admins

Pending items appear under the notification bell. Approve or reject each one from there. On Android, alerts also appear in the notification bar, so allow notifications for Chanda to receive them.

## Edits

Donations and expenses can be edited. An edit by someone who is not an admin needs admin approval before it applies.

## Archive requests

When someone who is not an admin asks to archive a donation, expense or member, an admin must approve the request. See [Archive and restore](/docs/archive-and-restore).

## Notifications

On mobile, notifications have their own screen. On desktop, the notification drawer is wider. Use "Mark all read" to clear pending requests from the list.

Every change is also recorded in the [Activity logs](/docs/activity-logs).
