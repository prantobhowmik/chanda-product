The public live count is a separate web page where anyone can follow a team's collection and expenses without signing in.

## How to get access

1. Ask the committee for the team ID.
2. The committee shares the team ID, or a ready-made link.
3. Open the link, or enter the team ID on the public page.

A team link looks like this:

```
https://chanda-public.vercel.app/<team id>
```

The public page is at [chanda-public.vercel.app](https://chanda-public.vercel.app/).

## What you see

New collections and expenses appear as the committee records them. The page shows donors, spending and the remaining balance for each festival.

## Sharing

Anyone who has the team ID or link can open the page, so the committee decides who gets it. Share the link only with people who should see the team's accounts.
