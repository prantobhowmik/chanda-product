Chanda is an offline-first app for community committees to record donations and expenses and keep everyone's books in sync. Entries save on the phone first, so the app never waits on the network.

## Where to use Chanda

- **Web app.** Open it in any modern browser from the [Download page](/download).
- **Android.** Install the APK from the [Download page](/download). Chanda is not on the Play Store.
- **iOS.** Coming soon.

## The basic flow

1. Sign in. Internet is needed for sign in, sync and backup. Recording works offline after sign in.
2. Create a [festival](/docs/festivals) for your fund or event.
3. Invite your members by email. See [Roles and invites](/docs/roles-and-invites).
4. [Record donations and expenses](/docs/recording-entries) as they happen.
5. Admins [approve or reject](/docs/approvals) submissions and edits, and watch the balance. The [Activity logs](/docs/activity-logs) show what changed.
6. [Export reports](/docs/reports-and-export) as PDF or Excel files.
