The Activity Logs screen shows what has happened in your team. Open it from the avatar menu.

## What is logged

- Direct edits.
- Archives and restores.

Earlier activity logs were rebuilt, so past history shows up too.

See [Approvals](/docs/approvals) and [Archive and restore](/docs/archive-and-restore) for how those changes are made.
