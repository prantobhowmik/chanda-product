## Is Chanda on the Play Store?

No. On Android it is a direct APK download from the [Download page](/download).

## Is there an iOS app?

Not yet. iOS is coming soon. The web app is available now in any modern browser.

## Does Chanda work without internet?

Yes. Entries save on the phone first. Internet is needed for sign in, sync and backup only, and recording works offline after sign in.

## Can I delete a donation, expense or member?

Chanda archives instead of deleting. Admins can restore archived items. See [Archive and restore](/docs/archive-and-restore).

## Which payment methods can I record?

Cash, bKash, Nagad and bank.

## Can I enter a date in the future?

No. Dates cannot be in the future.

## How do I move to a new phone?

Sign in on the new device and all your records are restored.

## Does Chanda have a dark theme?

Yes. Choose light, dark or system theme.

## What licence is Chanda under?
